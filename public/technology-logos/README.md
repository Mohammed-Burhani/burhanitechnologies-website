# Technology artwork

Local SVG assets used with the existing technology labels. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. The assets identify technologies; their presence does not imply endorsement or partnership.

- Devicon assets retain their upstream brand colors and geometry. Sources are pinned to v2.17.0; the MIT license is included.
- The .NET asset comes from the official .NET brand repository at the commit below. Its license is included.
- The Power BI asset comes from Microsoft's repository at the commit below. Credit: Microsoft, PowerBI-Icons, CC BY 4.0. Its license is included; trademark rights remain with the owner.
- The Tableau path comes from Simple Icons 11.15.0 at the commit below. Its upstream color metadata (#E97627) is applied; the CC0 license is included.
- UiPath uses the current dark corporate wordmark from its official homepage, captured 22 October 2026. See its [brand guidelines](https://www.uipath.com/assets/downloads/uipath-brand-guidelines).
- Automation Anywhere uses the current standalone icon from its official website. No open-license claim is made for UiPath or Automation Anywhere artwork.
- Category/protocol symbols are original neutral diagrams, not third-party brand marks.
- React Native reuses the React mark while retaining its separate text label.

SVG processing removes XML declarations, comments, and inter-element whitespace. No fonts, scripts, or remotely loaded rendering resources are included.
